ånde / a free little guide

From the mat
to the map.

A little yoga, a little sunshine, and nowhere you need to be. Three simple outfit ideas for the days that start slowly and take you somewhere new.

01 / Slow morning

Begin with movement.

Start with the clothes you already feel comfortable stretching in. Keep a breathable layer nearby for the walk home or a cooler morning.

02 / Out the door

Pack a small change.

A simple white tee, your favorite relaxed bottoms, and comfortable walking shoes can make the transition from class to café feel effortless.

03 / Open horizons

Leave room to wander.

Bring a lightweight outer layer, water, and a compact bag for the essentials. Check the weather and terrain before heading out.
