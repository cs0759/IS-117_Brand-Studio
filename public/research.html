ånde / IS117-007 / research before building

A tee with a point of view.

A dated, source-linked comparison of three clothing websites, two possible brand positions, and the audience ånde proposes to serve. This is design research, not customer validation.

Question Three sites Positions Audiences Decision trail Peer review
01 / Scope

Research question

How should ånde present a white baby-tee concept to a visitor who moves between yoga, everyday errands, and outdoor adventures, without letting the atmospheric branding obscure useful shopping information?

Method and date: Desk review of original brand-owned category pages on November 10, 2026. Observations describe visible navigation, categorization and product information; they are not user testing or independent verification of manufacturers' claims. Product prices, offers and assortments can change.

Prior work reused: ånde storefront, live style guide, and free styling guide. The site currently uses fictional product details and generated concept photography, not an actual purchasable garment.

02 / Sources

Three sites, three approaches

Each source is the clothing company's own page, not a third-party summary.

01 / Patagonia — activity and material guidance

Original source: Patagonia, Women's T-Shirts . Reviewed November 10, 2026.

Observed: The category page offers filters for fit, material and sport, and distinguishes technical from natural-fiber tees. Product listings label features such as quick drying or organic cotton.

Interpretation: Shoppers can approach a tee through intended activity as well as appearance. This suggests that ånde should clarify the imagined wearing situation, but should not borrow claims about fabric performance or sustainability.

02 / Outdoor Voices — casual-to-active assortment

Original source: Outdoor Voices, Women's Tops & Tanks . Reviewed November 10, 2026.

Observed: The page groups everyday shirts, baby tees and workout tops in one browsable collection, with sorting, price filters and visible product options.

Interpretation: The mix supports a possible active-to-everyday story. It does not prove that visitors actually wear those garments interchangeably.

03 / UNIQLO — fit and straightforward basics

Original source: UNIQLO US, Women's T-Shirts & Tank Tops . Reviewed November 10, 2026.

Observed: The category includes filters for size, color, sleeve and fit, along with sections such as cropped, slim and relaxed. It foregrounds straightforward product comparisons.

Interpretation: An editorial brand still benefits from unambiguous fit and size information. The ånde concept currently has a size selector, but no verified fit measurements.

Limit: source observations were checked against the linked original pages; no participant interviews, conversion data, physical garment examination or comparative usability test has occurred.

03 / Compare

Two positions

A / Everyday essential

Lead with a versatile white tee, a plain product view and clear sizing. Strong at helping someone compare basics, but risks becoming indistinct from established essentials retailers.

B / Explorer lifestyle

Lead with a poetic, forest-oriented mood and the journey from movement to travel. Distinctive for the ånde concept, but photography could bury practical product information.

Provisional choice / B with A's clarity

Keep the atmospheric Explorer identity already chosen in Brand Studio, with prominent size selection, a demo-only disclaimer, and a short factual product-information area.

Brand character and look: The chosen Explorer archetype expresses curiosity, freedom and unhurried movement. The visual direction is a restrained postmodern editorial composition: misty woodland photography, ivory background, serif typography and layered imagery. This is an existing learner-selected design direction, not a finding proved by competitor research.

Honest persuasion: Keep the free yoga-to-travel guide (reciprocity) and the optional slow-morning/open-horizons choice (commitment and consistency). Avoid fabricated reviews, countdowns, urgency or unverified textile and performance claims.

04 / Hypotheses

Two possible visitors

Primary proposal / Yoga-to-everyday explorer

Situation: Wants one relaxed look for a morning class, coffee and an unplanned outing. Goal: Feel comfortable and expressive without buying a different outfit for every activity. Concern: Does not know whether a styled garment will suit everyday wear. Page task: Understand the concept, view styling suggestions and identify the available demo size.

Status: Audience hypothesis; no customer interviews or measured demand. Selected provisionally because it connects the earlier ånde brief to the existing yoga-to-travel guide.

Secondary proposal / Product-first basics shopper

Situation: Comparing plain white tees across sites. Goal: Find exact fit, material and price details quickly. Concern: Lifestyle imagery may conceal important specifications. Page task: Distinguish known facts from placeholders and navigate to sizing.

Status: Contrasting hypothesis. It exposes a weakness in the current concept: garment specifications have not been verified, so a real-store fit guide cannot yet be promised.

05 / Traceability

Finding to proposed decision

Trace A / Product clarity

Evidence: UNIQLO's category exposes fit and size choices (source 03). Assumption: An ånde visitor will also value quick orientation. Proposal: Preserve the conspicuous size picker and clearly mark fictional product details; if a real garment is sourced later, add verified measurements. Check to run: Ask a peer to locate size selection and identify whether they understand that checkout is only a classroom demo. This check has not happened.

Trace B / Contextual styling

Evidence: Outdoor Voices shows everyday and workout shirts together (source 02), while Patagonia organizes garments by activity (source 01). Assumption: ånde's proposed primary visitor may want suggestions across these situations. Proposal: Retain the free Yoga to Travel guide, rather than adding unsupported technical garment claims. Check to run: Ask the reviewer to name one concrete useful takeaway from the guide and whether it feels relevant without a purchase. Not yet performed.

Current implementation versus proposal

The homepage size selector and styling guide predate this research. Research supports keeping or revisiting those choices; it is not evidence that research caused their original implementation. No new storefront feature has been built for IS117-007.
